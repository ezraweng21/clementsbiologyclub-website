# BioBox sponsor and fundraising playbook

Updated 2026-08-17. This is an officer working guide. Confirm all program windows, eligibility, and district approval before submitting. A student club normally needs the faculty sponsor, school activity fund, PTO, education foundation, or another eligible nonprofit to receive funds.

## First: qualify a lead in five minutes

Score each prospect before spending time on it:

1. Does it explicitly support K-12 STEM, youth learning, lab materials, or community health?
2. Does it serve Fort Bend County, Sugar Land, Houston, or Texas?
3. Is its typical gift realistic for a $250-$2,500 student project?
4. Can a school/PTO/fiscal sponsor receive the money?
5. Will it fund supplies, prizes, or event costs - not only staff salaries?
6. Is there a simple local request, rolling window, or near-term deadline?
7. Can BioBox provide a specific outcome: kits, students served, teacher feedback, or competition submissions?
8. What reporting/photo/logo acknowledgement does it expect?
9. Does it need a tax receipt, W-9, purchase order, or faculty signature?
10. Is there a named local manager, community-relations person, or official form?

Prioritize leads with 8+ “yes” answers. Do not mass-email generic national grant inboxes.

### Short initial sponsor email

**Subject:** Sponsor a hands-on science experience for Fort Bend students

Hello [Name],

I’m [Name] from Clements Biology Club. We are seeking a [specific amount/item] sponsor for BioBox, a student-led program that delivers hands-on, TEKS-aligned science lessons to local classrooms. A [gift] would serve approximately [number] students through [specific activity] and receive [brief recognition, if approved]. Our faculty sponsor can provide the school payment/receipt process and an impact recap. Would this be a fit for your community-support program?

Thank you,
[Name] | Clements Biology Club | [faculty sponsor contact]

### Follow-up discipline

- Follow up once after five business days and once after 10 more days.
- Do not send attachments on the first email unless asked; link a one-page overview instead.
- Record every touchpoint and response. Stop after two unanswered follow-ups.
- For a “no,” offer the Teacher Grant Help Program or a lower-cost in-kind request.

## Crowdfunding and community events

- **DonorsChoose:** strongest for teacher-owned classroom supply needs; BioBox can provide the TEKS, materials list, photos of supplies, and a short project description.
- **School-approved GoFundMe / Givebutter / PTO page:** use only if the sponsor confirms the school’s financial policy. Set one goal such as “$750 for 10 low-cost BioBox class kits,” publish the shopping list, and report spending afterward.
- **Sponsor-a-kit campaign:** $25 supplies a full low-cost class activity; $100 funds four activities; $250 funds a named school delivery. Offer acknowledgement only with school approval.
- **BioThon:** use free entry or waivers, optional community sponsorship, and a confirmed prize pool before public announcement. The supplied BioThon plan recommends a Year-1 volume-first model with $200/$100/$50 prizes and a simple, local-data-focused proposal format.
- **Article awards:** seek one $25 gift card sponsor per month and one $50 annual prize sponsor; this is a clean ask for bookstores, cafes, tutoring centers, and parent employers.
- **Auction / trivia / science night:** use donated experiences, gift cards, or small business baskets rather than purchasing inventory upfront.

## Lead tracker fields

Use a shared sheet with: Organization | Local location/contact | Official request route | Contact name | Email/form URL | Qualification score | Specific ask | Category | Date contacted | Follow-up 1 | Follow-up 2 | Outcome | Amount/item | Reporting due | Notes.

## What to report back to every funder

- Exact dollars or items received and what was purchased.
- Number of classes, teachers, and students served.
- The named BioBox activities and TEKS/unit connection.
- One teacher quote and optional, permission-compliant photo of materials/student work.
- What will happen next because of the gift.

That short impact packet makes the next grant and sponsorship ask much easier.
