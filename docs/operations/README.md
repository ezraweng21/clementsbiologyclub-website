# Club operations workspace

This folder is the repository-side companion to the Google operating plan and Master Sheet.

## Structure

- `biothon/` - rules, forms, judge onboarding, and launch checklist.
- `biobox/` - teacher pilots, facilitator plans, inventory, and impact records.
- `teaching/` - AAC/AP lesson-plan template, question bank process, and feedback summaries.
- `partnerships/` - one-page partner asks, outreach logs, and meeting notes.
- `communications/` - Bio Bites calendar, event recap template, and approved media checklist.

The Google **Master Sheet 2026-2027** is the source for live status; its new **Workstreams** tab defines the owner and first deliverable for every major program.
