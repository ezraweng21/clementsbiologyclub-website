# BioBox purchasing guide

This is a purchasing guide, not a promise of a particular vendor or price. Compare the classroom-pack price, shipping, storage, and exact class count before ordering. Keep the default catalog ceiling at **$35 per class**.

## Buy from Carolina Biological when it adds real classroom value

| Activity or supply | What to buy there | Why Carolina is worth it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agar Plates and Microbial Culture | Sterile prepared agar plates, sterile swabs, teacher-approved safe culture materials | Sterility, packaging, and instructional guidance matter. Do not substitute uncontrolled home cultures. |
| PTC Taste Test Strips | Classroom PTC paper strips | Consistent concentration and individually usable strips make comparison fairer. |
| Brine Shrimp Life Cycle | Brine shrimp eggs/cyst culture supplies and optional culture dishes | A reliable education supplier makes the live-organism setup more predictable. |
| Cheek Cell Microscopy | Prepared slides, microscope stains, coverslips, and disposable droppers if school inventory is short | Good for durable, reusable lab supplies; do **not** buy a microscope for this kit by default. |
| Iodine Macromolecule Test | Classroom iodine solution, spot plates, droppers | Small science-pack quantities and labeled containers are useful if the school has none. |
| Owl Pellet Dissection | Sanitized pellets, tools, bone charts | Use Carolina only if grant-funded or donated: the standard class kit exceeds the $35-per-class ceiling. |
| Blood Typing Simulation | Synthetic blood-typing simulation materials, if the teacher chooses it | Safer and clearer than trying to improvise anything resembling real blood. |

## Usually buy elsewhere

These activities are strongest when supplies are bought locally, in bulk, or from a specialized source rather than Carolina: Elephant Toothpaste; cabbage pH; gummy-bear osmosis; DNA extraction; yeast fermentation; density column; natural-selection simulation; seed germination; balloon lungs; surface tension; leaf chromatography; soil testing; mealworms; diffusion; crystal growing; heart-rate investigation; DIY stethoscope; penny surface tension; vinegar/baking soda; oobleck; pollinator structure; paper PCR; Punnett squares; vitamin-C titration consumables; algae/data models; bioplastic; and bioinformatics.

- **Grocery/discount/bulk:** yeast, peroxide (teacher-approved concentration), cabbage, baking soda, vinegar, food coloring, gelatin/gummy bears, corn syrup, oil, salt, coffee filters, seeds, balloons, cups, pennies, and pantry containers.
- **School inventory first:** microscopes, balances, timers, hot plates, graph paper, basic glassware, goggles, and computers.
- **Pet or local science supplier:** mealworms. Buy only the amount needed for the planned observation period and confirm care/disposal with the teacher.
- **Specialist/direct supplier:** Foldscope from Foldscope; pGLO and authentic gel electrophoresis from Bio-Rad; water-quality reagents from the teacher’s preferred approved water-testing vendor. pGLO and Foldscope are not in the normal under-$35 catalog.

## Order quantities and deal checks

1. Ask the teacher for sections, average students per section, group size, storage constraints, and whether any supplies already exist.
2. Price **per student group**, not only per kit. For a 30-student class working in groups of four, plan for 8–10 stations plus 10–15% spare consumables.
3. Check shipping and minimum orders before comparing unit price. Carolina is most useful when a science-only order combines several sterile, live-organism, or reusable-lab items.
4. For generic consumables, compare a school-pack unit cost with grocery, restaurant-supply, and bulk retailer options. Avoid a “classroom kit” if it merely repackages pantry ingredients.
5. Buy reusable items once (spot plates, droppers, trays, goggles) and refill consumables later. Record vendor, SKU, class count, purchase date, and delivered cost in the BioBox inventory sheet.
6. For live or time-sensitive materials, order only after a date is confirmed and leave a delivery buffer. Do not order cultures, animals, or reagents without the teacher’s safety/handling approval.

## Carolina order shortlist

If building one initial Carolina cart, prioritize **sterile agar plates/swabs, PTC strips, brine shrimp eggs, inexpensive slides/spot plates/droppers, iodine solution**, and only simulation materials the teacher specifically requests. Do not use Carolina as the default store for household demonstrations.
